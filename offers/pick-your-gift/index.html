How a pick-your-gift offer works

1

Set the qualifying trigger. Cart total, a specific product, or a collection — for example, spend $60 and the gift picker unlocks.

2

Build the gift set. Choose two to four products as the eligible gifts. Each one is still a real catalog product, so inventory and variants stay accurate.

3

Shopper chooses. The cart widget shows the options with images and names. One click adds the chosen gift at $0, styled to match your theme.

4

Track per gift. Funnel analytics break out acceptance by gift, so you see which option shoppers actually pick and which one just sits there.

Why a choice beats a fixed gift

A fixed gift has one failure mode: the shopper does not want that item. The offer then reads as noise, and the spend threshold loses its pull. Giving a choice removes the mismatch without adding cost — you still control which products are eligible, so every option is one you are happy to give away at your own margin.

The second benefit is data. When three gifts compete in the same slot, the pick rate tells you which product has genuine pull with your buyers. Stores commonly use that signal to decide what to sample, what to bundle, and what to retire. A single fixed gift can never produce that comparison.

Pick-your-gift also protects price integrity. Like any gift with purchase offer, nothing on the core product is discounted — the incentive sits beside the purchase instead of inside the price.

Shopify gifting mechanics worth knowing

Shopify's native discount engine can add a free product through a Buy X Get Y rule, but it has no concept of a shopper-selected gift: the Y is fixed when you create the rule. To let the shopper choose, something has to render the options in the cart and swap the line item on selection. That is the gap an app fills.

  • Inventory. Gifts draw from real stock. Keep a buffer on anything you offer as a gift during a promotion, or exclude low-stock items from the set.
  • Margin. Price the threshold off the gift's cost, not its retail price. A $12-cost gift unlocked at $60 behaves very differently from the same gift unlocked at $25.
  • Returns. Decide up front whether a returned order requires the gift back. Most stores do not chase low-cost gifts, but the policy should exist before the campaign.
  • One gift per order. Unless you intend a gift ladder, cap the offer at one claim so a shopper cannot stack several gifts on one qualifying cart.

Frequently asked questions

What is a pick-your-gift offer?

A pick-your-gift offer is a gift-with-purchase promotion where the shopper chooses their free gift from a set of options you approve, instead of receiving one fixed gift. The qualifying condition is the same — usually a minimum cart value, a specific product, or a collection — but the shopper selects which gift gets added at $0.

Can Shopify do pick-your-gift natively?

No. Shopify's native Buy X Get Y discount can add a free product, but the free product is fixed when you create the discount. There is no built-in way for a shopper to choose between several gifts in the cart, so this needs an app that renders the options and adds the selected gift as a $0 line item.

How many gift options should I offer?

Two to four. One is not a choice, and more than four turns a quick decision into a browsing task inside the cart, which is where you least want hesitation. Pick options at similar cost to you so margin stays predictable whichever one the shopper takes.

Does the gift come out of my real inventory?

Yes. Gifts are normal catalog products added at zero cost, so stock levels and variants stay accurate and fulfilment works as usual. Exclude anything low on stock from the gift set before launching a campaign.

Can I see which gift shoppers choose most?

Yes. NUO AI's funnel analytics report acceptance per gift, so you can see pick rate per option alongside the offer's view count and the revenue of the carts that claimed it — which is also a useful read on which products have pull with your buyers.
