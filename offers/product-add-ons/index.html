Product add-ons

Product add-ons for Shopify, without burning variants.

A product add-on is an optional extra attached to something the shopper is already buying — gift wrap, express assembly, a two-year warranty, a spare filter. It is the highest-margin line on most orders, and it does not need to live in your variant matrix. NUO AI offers add-ons on the product page and in the cart, priced and tracked separately, free.

Why add-ons should not be variants

The reflex on Shopify is to model an extra as a variant — "Blue / Large / With gift wrap". It works until it does not. Shopify allows a limited number of options and variant combinations per product, and every add-on you encode as an option multiplies the matrix. Three colours, four sizes and two wrap choices is 24 variants, most of which exist only to carry a $4 upcharge.

The damage goes past the count. Variant-encoded add-ons pollute inventory tracking, break collection filters, distort reporting, and make the product page's option selectors harder to read. And a shopper who has to re-pick their size because they changed their mind about gift wrap is a shopper one step closer to leaving.

An add-on layer keeps the extra as its own priced line that attaches to the chosen variant, so the variant matrix stays about the product and the extras stay about the order. Add-ons that reliably sell

  • Gift wrap and a gift message. The default starting add-on. High attach rate in Q4, trivial cost, and it tells you which orders are gifts.
  • Protection and warranty. Strong on electronics, furniture and anything fragile. Margin is high, but be precise about what is covered.
  • Personalisation. Engraving, embroidery, a printed name. Capture the text at the point of sale so fulfilment is not chasing it by email later.
  • Consumables and spares. Extra blades, filters, pods, refills — the shopper will buy them eventually, and the cheapest moment to sell them is now.
  • Service upgrades. Priority dispatch, installation, assembly. Only offer these where operations can actually deliver them.

One add-on prompt per product, two at most. Add-on menus are where carts go to stall.

Frequently asked questions

What is a product add-on?

A product add-on is an optional extra a shopper can attach to a product they are already buying — gift wrap, engraving, a warranty, installation, a spare part. It is not a standalone purchase, and it is usually priced far above its cost, which makes it one of the highest-margin lines on an order.

Why not just use Shopify variants for add-ons?

Variants multiply. Each add-on encoded as a product option combines with every colour and size you already sell, which inflates the variant matrix, muddies inventory and reporting, and makes the option selectors harder to use. An add-on layer attaches the extra as its own priced line instead, leaving the variant matrix to describe the product.

Can I offer free add-ons, like a gift message?

Yes. Add-ons can be priced, free, or a percentage of the item price, so a $0 gift message and a $49 warranty can run on the same product.

Where do add-ons appear?

On the product page next to the buy button, in the cart or cart drawer, or both. The widget inherits your theme's typography, spacing and button styling, so no theme edits are needed.

Can I offer add-ons at checkout?

Checkout is Shopify's most restricted surface and what you can place there depends on your plan. For most stores the cart is the practical place to offer paid extras; see our guide to add-ons at checkout on Shopify for what each surface allows.

How do I know which add-ons are working?

Funnel analytics report attach rate and revenue for each add-on, alongside the orders it appeared on. Low-attach add-ons are worth removing — every extra prompt costs attention on the way to checkout.
