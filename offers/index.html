Why run every promotion type from one app

Most stores end up stitching together a separate app for gifts, another for BOGO, and a third for upsells — each with its own analytics silo and its own theme-compatibility risk. NUO AI runs all of it through the same adaptive widget system and the same funnel analytics, so a discount-cost number for a gift campaign is directly comparable to the revenue lift from an AI upsell campaign, in one dashboard.

Every offer type on this page is free to launch, styled to match your store automatically, and measured with the same funnel: offer viewed → offer accepted → checkout started → order placed.
