Selleasy alternatives, and the cap problem nobody mentions.

Selleasy's free plan is one of the better ones on the App Store: 50 orders a month, renewing. The catch is in the wording — it counts your store's total order count, not orders Selleasy influenced. A store can lose the free plan purely from growth the app had nothing to do with.

Why merchants look for Selleasy alternatives

Selleasy rates 4.9 across 2,525 reviews and its free plan renews monthly rather than expiring, which already puts it ahead of several apps in this category. Merchants still look around for two reasons.

The cap counts your whole store. Selleasy's listing is explicit: "Up to 50 orders/mo. Your store's total order count will be considered." So the app can be influencing almost nothing and still push you past the cap, because the rest of the business is doing well. An app that counts only its own attributed orders — or its own generated revenue, as Essential does — behaves very differently as you grow.

Recommendation breadth is not promotional depth. Selleasy's strength is frequently-bought-together recommendations across the product page, cart, and post-purchase. If what you need is a spend-threshold free gift, a BOGO ladder, or a bundle builder, that is a different category of logic, and a recommendation engine is the wrong tool rather than a weaker one.

Worth noting what is not a reason: price. At $9/mo, Selleasy's first paid tier is among the cheapest in the set, so stores that outgrow the free plan are not facing a $29.99 or $54.99 step.

Frequently asked questions

Is Selleasy's free plan really free?

Yes, and it renews every month rather than expiring once — which is unusual in this category. The constraint is the unit: the 50-order cap counts your store's total orders, not the orders Selleasy influenced.

Why does Selleasy's cap counting total store orders matter?

Because general business growth can end your free plan even if the app is contributing almost nothing. Apps that cap on attributed orders or generated revenue only charge you when they have done something.

What is the cheapest Selleasy alternative?

On first paid tier, Upsell.com at $4.99/mo is the lowest in this set, though its free plan is limited to development stores. Wiser AI matches Selleasy at $9/mo with a comparable monthly order cap.

Does Selleasy do free gifts and BOGO?

Its listed strength is frequently-bought-together recommendations across product, cart, and post-purchase. Promotional mechanics like spend-threshold gifts and BOGO ladders are a different category of logic — check the current listing if that is your requirement.

Selleasy or Wiser AI?

The free caps are near-identical — 50 orders a month either way, $9/mo above it. The split is in approach: Selleasy spreads one recommendation engine across three placements, Wiser AI emphasises AI-driven recommendation matching. Our Selleasy vs Wiser AI page covers the detail.
