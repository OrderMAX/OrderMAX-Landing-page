Best discount stacking apps for Shopify, compared

Four of the five apps that show up on “best discount stacking app” lists have a free plan — and one of those four can't actually be used on a live store. The rest cap in three different units — number of offers, number of live variants, and monthly orders. Every figure below was read from that app’s own Shopify App Store listing on the same day and quoted verbatim.

The short answer

Dealeasy caps its free plan at 5 offers, fine for a single stacked promotion. Discounty caps at 10 live discounted variants and 3 campaigns. Kite resets a 50-order allowance every month. AIOD — despite appearing on nearly every stacking roundup — restricts its free tier to development stores only, so it cannot run on a live storefront at all. NUO AI, our own app disclosed below, states no cap, with a much shorter review history than the other four.

What ends each free plan

Combining more than one discount type on an order — a volume break plus a code, say — is the feature all five apps compete on. What this comparison does not tell you

Three things it deliberately does not claim, because the evidence for them does not exist:

It does not measure margin impact across apps. How much a stacked discount actually costs depends entirely on your own rules, not which app enforces them. None of these apps makes stacking free of margin risk — they make it easier to configure and track.

It does not verify native-code-plus-automatic behavior side by side. Shopify's own rules for how codes and automatic discounts combine have changed in recent years; AIOD and Discounty both advertise support for this, but exact behavior on your theme should be tested before launch, not assumed from the listing copy.

Pricing goes stale, and currency display is regional. Everything here was read on 3 November 2026. Frequently asked questions

Which Shopify discount stacking apps actually have a free plan?

As of 3 November 2026, three of the five widely recommended discount stacking apps have a genuine $0 plan usable on a live store: NUO AI, Kite and Dealeasy. Discounty's free plan is also real but narrow. AIOD's free tier is restricted to development stores and cannot be used on a live storefront.

What does discount stacking actually mean?

Discount stacking means combining more than one discount mechanic on the same order — for example, a volume break plus a percentage-off code, or a free-gift trigger layered on top of a sitewide sale. Done without a policy, it can compound into a margin loss nobody budgeted for, which is why every app in this comparison frames stacking as a controlled feature, not an accident.

What actually ends a free discount stacking app plan?

Three different units among the apps with a usable free plan. Dealeasy caps at 5 offers. Discounty caps at 10 live discounted variants and 3 campaigns. Kite caps at 50 discount orders a month, resetting monthly. NUO AI states no cap. AIOD has no usable free plan at all on a live store.

Is AIOD's free plan usable on a real store?

No. AIOD's listing states its free tier is for development stores only. A live storefront starts on a paid tier from roughly $9.99/month. It still appears on ‘best discount stacking app’ roundups because the paid product itself is well-reviewed, not because the free tier applies to a real store.

Which free discount stacking app has the most room before it caps out?

For a store running one or two active promotions, Kite's monthly-resetting 50-order cap tends to go furthest before billing starts, since it isn't limited by a fixed count of offers or variants the way Dealeasy and Discounty are. NUO AI has no cap at all, with the trade-off of a much shorter review history.

Can native Shopify discount codes be combined with app-based stacking?

Yes, on most apps in this comparison, including AIOD and Discounty, which explicitly advertise combining native discount codes with automatic app-based offers. Confirm this specifically before relying on it, since Shopify's own discount-combination rules changed how codes and automatic discounts interact in recent years.
