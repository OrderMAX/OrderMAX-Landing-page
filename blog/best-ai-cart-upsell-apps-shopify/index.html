Best AI cart upsell apps for Shopify, compared

“AI cart upsell” gets attached to a lot of listings; not all of them actually include AI on the free plan. Of the four apps compared here, one gates the AI behind a paid tier entirely. The other three cap in two different units — monthly orders and active campaigns. Every figure below was read from that app’s own Shopify App Store listing on the same day and quoted verbatim.

The short answer

XB AI Cart Upsell includes its AI product-matching free, capped at 50 monthly store orders. Selleasy brands an AI-driven engine across 18 strategies, free up to 5 active campaigns with unlimited orders. Carty's free plan is a sticky add-to-cart bar only — its AI upsell recommendations are a paid-tier feature. NUO AI, our own app disclosed below, includes AI-matched cart recommendations with no stated cap, with a much shorter review history than the other three. (Rebuy, a name that still shows up on most roundups, currently has no live Shopify App Store listing at all — it is absent from the comparison below for that reason.)

Frequently asked questions

Which Shopify AI cart upsell apps actually have a free plan that includes the AI?

As of 3 November 2026, three of the four apps in this comparison include AI-driven recommendations on their free plan: NUO AI, XB AI Cart Upsell, and Selleasy. Carty's free plan covers only a sticky add-to-cart bar — its AI upsell engine is gated behind the paid Growth tier.

What actually ends a free AI cart upsell app plan?

Two different units among the apps that include AI free. XB caps at 50 monthly store orders. Selleasy caps at 5 active campaigns with unlimited orders. NUO AI states no cap. Carty's free plan has no AI to cap — the AI itself only exists on its paid tier.

Is Carty's free plan an AI cart upsell plan?

No. Carty's free plan includes a sticky add-to-cart bar and sticky cart button only. The listing's AI-powered upsell recommendations activate on the paid Growth tier, priced at roughly $0.05 per order until 2,000 orders, then free. Installing Carty for its free plan alone does not get you the AI.

Why isn't Rebuy in this comparison?

Because its Shopify App Store listing currently shows as not available, as of 3 November 2026. No live rating or pricing could be verified for a delisted app, so it is named here as a known absence rather than included with stale or unverifiable figures. Check the listing directly, since app availability has reportedly changed before.

Does AI cart upsell actually outperform rule-based upsell logic?

The pattern is well reasoned — matching offers to real cart contents should outperform a static, manually configured rule set as catalogs grow — but no independent, controlled comparison between AI-matched and rule-based upsells on identical traffic was found publicly for this comparison. Treat it as a reasonable expectation, not a verified percentage.

Why does NUO AI appear in its own comparison?

Because leaving it out would be its own kind of bias. NUO AI publishes this comparison and is one of the four apps in it.
