Best BOGO apps for Shopify, compared

Four of the five apps that show up on every “best BOGO app” list have a free plan. The fifth doesn’t. The four that do are capped in three different units — lifetime orders, monthly orders, and active campaigns plus revenue. Every figure below was read from that app’s own Shopify App Store listing on the same day and quoted verbatim.

The short answer

BOGOS is the most established BOGO app by review count, but its free tier is 30 lifetime orders — an extended trial, not an ongoing plan. Kite resets its 50-order cap every month, making it the most sustainable free option for low volume. AOV.ai caps at 2 active campaigns and $500 of monthly app revenue. NUO AI — our own app, disclosed below — states no cap. Vitals, which appears on nearly every BOGO roundup, has no free plan at all: a 7-day trial, then roughly $29.99/month as part of a 40-tool bundle.

What ends each free plan

What this comparison does not tell you

Three things it deliberately does not claim, because the evidence for them does not exist:

It does not rank the apps by conversion lift. No one has run these five apps against the same catalog, traffic and BOGO structure. It does not verify feature parity beyond the cap. “Multiplied BOGO” and “cheapest item free” mean similar things across these apps but were not tested side by side. The caps were verifiable from each listing in an afternoon; deep feature behavior was not.

Frequently asked questions

Which Shopify BOGO apps actually have a free plan?

As of 3 November 2026, four of the five widely recommended BOGO apps have a genuine $0 plan: NUO AI, BOGOS, Kite and AOV.ai. The fifth, Vitals, does not — its BOGO tool only exists inside a paid all-in-one subscription with a 7-day trial and no ongoing free tier.

What is the difference between BOGO and Buy X Get Y?

BOGO (buy one get one) is the simplest case of the broader Buy X Get Y mechanic, where buying a set quantity (X) unlocks a reward quantity (Y) at a discount or free. Every app in this comparison supports both under one feature set, since BOGO is just BXGY with X and Y both equal to 1.

What actually ends a free BOGO app plan?

Three different units among the apps that have a free plan at all. BOGOS counts lifetime orders (30, spent once). Kite counts monthly orders that reset every month (50). AOV.ai counts active campaigns (2) plus a $500 monthly revenue cap. NUO AI states no cap. Vitals has no free plan to compare against these.

Is Vitals a free BOGO app?

No. Vitals bundles BOGO inside its all-in-one subscription, which carries a 7-day free trial and then bills from roughly $29.99/month. It appears on BOGO roundups because the feature itself is good, not because the plan is free.

Which free BOGO app has the most generous cap?

Kite's 50-orders-a-month cap resets every month rather than draining a lifetime allowance, which makes it the most sustainable ongoing free option among the capped apps for a low-volume store. NUO AI has no cap at all, with the trade-off of a much shorter review history.

Can BOGO offers run at the cart level, not just the product page?

Yes, on every app in this comparison. Cart-level BOGO tends to convert more reliably than product-page-only BOGO, because the shopper sees the reward item added automatically rather than having to notice an offer on a separate page.

Why does NUO AI appear in its own comparison?

Because leaving it out would be its own kind of bias. NUO AI publishes this comparison and is one of the five apps in it. Every competitor's pricing below was read from its own Shopify App Store listing on 3 November 2026 and quoted verbatim, and NUO AI's weakest point — a short review history against competitors with thousands — is stated rather than hidden.

How many App Store reviews does NUO AI have?

Far fewer than the other four apps on this page, which range from 1,370 to 4,643 reviews as of 3 November 2026. A merchant choosing on track record alone should weigh that heavily; this comparison does not argue otherwise.
