Frequently asked questions

Which Shopify product add-ons apps actually have a free plan?

As of 3 November 2026, three of the five widely recommended product add-ons apps have a free plan usable on a live store: NUO AI, Easify, and Aco. EPA and Kefi both restrict their free tier to development or pre-production stores only.

What is the difference between a product add-on and a variant?

A Shopify variant (size, color) is a version of the same product with its own SKU and inventory count. A priced add-on — gift wrap, a warranty, engraving — is a separate, optional extra that attaches to an order without multiplying variants. Using variants for add-ons causes the combinatorial "variant bloat" Shopify caps at 100 per product; a dedicated add-ons app avoids that entirely.

What actually ends a free product add-ons app plan?

Two different patterns. Aco caps at 10 products and 10 field types on its free plan. Easify's free plan is uncapped — unlimited option sets, products and orders. EPA and Kefi don't cap their free plan at all; they simply don't offer one for a live store. NUO AI states no cap.

Are EPA and Kefi's free plans usable on a real store?

No. Both listings state their free tier is for development or pre-production stores only. A live storefront on either app starts on a paid plan, with a 14 to 21-day trial. They still appear on "best product add-ons app" roundups because the paid product is well-reviewed, not because the free tier applies to a real store.

Is Easify's free plan really uncapped?

Its own listing states "Free Forever" with unlimited option sets, products and orders, and 15 option types included at $0. The paid tiers add more option types (28 vs 15), add-on products, and AI-readiness features rather than lifting an order or product cap, which is a different trade-off than apps that gate on volume.

Can product add-ons be priced extras rather than free customization fields?

Yes, on every app in this comparison. Each supports attaching a price to an option — gift wrap at $3, a warranty at $15 — in addition to free customization fields like engraving text or color choice.

Why does NUO AI appear in its own comparison?

Because leaving it out would be its own kind of bias. NUO AI publishes this comparison and is one of the five apps in it. Every competitor's pricing below was read from its own Shopify App Store listing on 3 November 2026 and quoted verbatim, and NUO AI's weakest point — a short review history, and a simpler add-ons feature than dedicated option-builder apps — is stated rather than hidden.
