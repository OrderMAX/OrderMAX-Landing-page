Best pick your gift apps for Shopify, compared

Not every gift-with-purchase app lets the shopper choose — many only auto-add one fixed gift. These five specifically support a gift-picker mode, and their free tiers are capped in four different units — live rules, attributed revenue, lifetime orders, and monthly orders. Every figure below was read from that app’s own Shopify App Store listing on the same day and quoted verbatim.

The short answer

EG Auto Add to Cart has the highest review count of the dedicated gift-picker apps at 1,156, but its free tier caps at a single live gift rule. TL Free Gifts ties its free plan to $1,000 of monthly revenue on Basic stores. BOGOS gives a 30-lifetime-order allowance that functions as a trial. Fokkio resets monthly at 10 orders. NUO AI — our own app, disclosed below — states no cap on its gift-picker widget, with a much shorter review history than the other four.

All five apps here let the shopper pick from an approved set of gifts rather than receiving one fixed item. The picker UI is close to interchangeable; the cap is what differs. Ratings and review counts are as shown on the Shopify App Store on 3 November 2026.

What this comparison does not tell you

Three things it deliberately does not claim, because the evidence for them does not exist:

It does not measure picker-vs-fixed conversion lift. No controlled test comparing a picker offer against a fixed gift on identical traffic was found publicly. The pattern — choice removes the “I don't want that” objection — is well reasoned, not independently verified at a specific percentage.

It does not verify picker UI quality side by side. All five apps show the picker as a popup or in-cart widget; actual look, load time and theme compatibility were not tested on a live store for this comparison.

Frequently asked questions

What is a pick-your-gift app?

A pick-your-gift app lets a shopper choose their own free gift from an approved set once they qualify, instead of receiving one fixed gift automatically. It's a variant of gift with purchase (GWP) — the mechanic is the same spend or quantity trigger, but the shopper gets a choice rather than a single predetermined item.

Which Shopify apps actually support shopper-chosen gifts, not just fixed GWP?

As of 3 November 2026, five widely used apps support a gift-picker mode where the shopper chooses: NUO AI, BOGOS, EG Auto Add to Cart, TL Free Gifts BOGO & Upsell, and Fokkio. Not every GWP app offers this — some only support one fixed gift per rule, so confirm the picker specifically before installing if shopper choice is the point.

What actually ends a free pick-your-gift app plan?

Four different units. EG Auto Add to Cart caps at 1 live gift rule. TL caps at $1,000 of monthly revenue on Shopify Basic stores. BOGOS gives 30 lifetime orders, spent once. Fokkio allows 10 orders a month, resetting monthly. NUO AI states no cap on any of these.

Does picking your own gift convert better than a fixed gift?

Letting a shopper choose removes the single most common objection to fixed GWP — "I don't want that specific item" — so acceptance rates are generally reported higher for picker-style offers across this category. No independent, controlled study comparing fixed vs. picker GWP head-to-head on identical traffic exists publicly, so treat this as a well-reasoned pattern, not a verified percentage.

Is EG Auto Add to Cart's free plan enough for an ongoing picker offer?

Only if you run exactly one gift rule. The free plan caps at "1 live gift rule," which is enough to run a single threshold with a choice of gifts, but not enough to run different picker offers for different collections or customer segments at the same time.

Far fewer. The other four apps on this page range from 85 to 4,643 reviews as of 3 November 2026. If track record matters more to you than cap flexibility, weigh that heavily — this page does not argue otherwise.

Can I limit which gifts a shopper can pick from?

Yes, across every app in this comparison. You set the approved gift set (typically 2 to 4 products) and the trigger that unlocks it; the shopper only ever sees and picks from the products you approved, not your full catalog.
