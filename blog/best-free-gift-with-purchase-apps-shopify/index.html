Best free gift with purchase (GWP) apps for Shopify, compared

“Best GWP app” and “best free gift with purchase app” are the same search — GWP is just the abbreviation. Six apps show up across both. Five of them have a genuinely free plan, capped in three different units — lifetime orders, monthly orders, and active campaigns plus revenue. The sixth doesn’t have a free plan at all. Every figure below was read from that app’s own Shopify App Store listing on the same day and quoted verbatim.

The short answer

Five of the six apps roundups call “best free GWP app” have a real $0 tier. BOGOS gives 30 lifetime orders — an extended trial rather than an ongoing plan. Kite and Fokkio reset monthly at 50 and 10 orders. AOV.ai caps at 2 active campaigns and $500 of monthly app revenue. NUO AI — our own app, disclosed below — states no cap. Vitals, which appears on nearly every “free GWP app” list, has no free plan at all: a 7-day trial, then roughly $29.99/month minimum as part of a 40-tool bundle.

Kite — the most sustainable ongoing free tier of the capped apps

4.9 · 1,370 reviews · Skai Lama

Kite runs free gifts, BOGO and tiered/volume discounts from one app built on native Shopify Functions, with redemption available on the product page, cart and checkout. Its free allowance resets every month rather than draining a one-time lifetime pool, which makes it the most sustainable free option among the apps that actually cap usage.

The cap is order-based, not campaign-based. The listing states “First 50 discount orders free each month,” after which it bills $29/month. A store doing 40 orders a month on this app stays free indefinitely; a store doing 80 pays from month one.

  • Next tier$29/mo once over 50 orders · higher tiers scale with store size, 7-day free trial

AOV.ai — AI-assisted campaigns, capped on two axes at once

What this comparison does not tell you

It does not rank the apps by AOV lift. No one has run these six apps against the same catalog, traffic and gift strategy. It does not verify feature parity beyond the cap. “Tiered gifts” and “gift-picker” mean similar things across these apps but were not tested side by side. The caps were verifiable from each listing in an afternoon; deep feature behavior was not.

Frequently asked questions

Which Shopify gift-with-purchase apps actually have a free plan?

As of 3 November 2026, five of the six widely recommended GWP apps have a genuine $0 plan: NUO AI, BOGOS, Kite, AOV.ai and Fokkio. The sixth, Vitals, does not — its free gift tool only exists inside a paid all-in-one subscription with a 7-day trial and no ongoing free tier.

What is the difference between ‘best GWP app’ and ‘best free gift with purchase app’?

Nothing meaningful — GWP is simply the abbreviation for gift with purchase, and both phrases describe the same category of app. This page covers both searches rather than splitting into two near-identical pages.

What actually ends a free GWP app plan?

Four different units. BOGOS counts lifetime orders (30, spent once). Fokkio and Kite count monthly orders that reset every month (10 and 50 respectively). AOV.ai counts active campaigns (2) plus a $500 monthly revenue cap. NUO AI states no cap on any of these. Vitals has no free plan at all to compare.

Is Vitals actually free for gift with purchase?

No. Vitals bundles a free-gift tool inside its all-in-one subscription, which carries a 7-day free trial and then bills from roughly $29.99/month. It appears on most ‘best free GWP app’ roundups because it offers a free gift feature, not a free gift plan. The distinction matters if budget is the reason you're reading this.

Which free GWP app has the most generous cap?

For a low-volume store, Kite's 50-orders-a-month cap resets every month rather than draining a lifetime allowance, which makes it the most sustainable ongoing free option among the capped apps. NUO AI has no cap at all, with the trade-off of a much shorter track record.

Can I let shoppers choose their own free gift?

Yes, on several of these apps, including NUO AI, BOGOS, Fokkio and AOV.ai — look for a ‘pick your gift’ or gift-picker widget setting rather than a single fixed gift. See our dedicated comparison of pick-your-gift apps for the apps that specialize in shopper choice specifically.

Why does NUO AI appear in its own comparison?

Because leaving it out would be its own kind of bias. NUO AI publishes this comparison and is one of the six apps in it. Every competitor's pricing below was read from its own Shopify App Store listing on 3 November 2026 and quoted verbatim, and NUO AI's weakest point — a short review history against competitors with thousands — is stated rather than hidden.

How many App Store reviews does NUO AI have?

Far fewer than the other five apps on this page, which range from 85 to 4,643 reviews as of 3 November 2026. A merchant choosing on track record alone should weigh that heavily; this comparison does not argue otherwise.

Does gift with purchase actually increase average order value?

The mechanism is well evidenced: giving shoppers a visible, concrete reason to cross a spend threshold is one of the most consistently cited AOV levers in ecommerce. The magnitude depends on your catalog, margin and the gift you choose, which is why starting on a free tier to test it is lower-risk than committing to a paid plan first.
