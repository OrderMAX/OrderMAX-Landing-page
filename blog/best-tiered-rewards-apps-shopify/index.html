Best tiered rewards apps for Shopify, compared

“Tiered rewards” covers two different mechanics that roundups routinely blur together. Cart-level tiers (NUO AI, Kite) unlock a reward instantly inside the current order. Loyalty-points tiers (Smile, BON, Appstle) unlock VIP status over an account's lifetime spend. All five have a free plan; every figure below was read from that app’s own Shopify App Store listing on the same day and quoted verbatim.

The short answer

For an instant, single-cart spend ladder, the real choice is between NUO AI (no cap) and Kite (50 discount orders a month, resetting monthly). For an account-level VIP loyalty tier, the real choice is between Smile (200 orders/mo free), BON (150 orders/mo free) and Appstle (250 orders/mo free) — all three cap on monthly order volume, not on tiers or points. These are not substitutes for each other; pick based on which mechanic you actually need.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between cart-level tiered rewards and tiered loyalty points?

Cart-level tiered rewards unlock instantly within a single order — spend $50 and get 10% off, spend $100 and get free shipping, all inside the current cart. Tiered loyalty programs unlock VIP status over an account's lifetime spend or visit history, independent of any single order. NUO AI and Kite run the first kind; Smile, BON and Appstle run the second.

Which Shopify tiered rewards apps actually have a free plan?

As of 3 November 2026, all five apps in this comparison have a genuine $0 plan: NUO AI, Kite, Smile, BON and Appstle. All four competitors cap on monthly order count; NUO AI states no cap.

What actually ends a free tiered rewards app plan?

All four competitors cap on monthly order volume, though the threshold differs: Kite allows 50 discount orders a month, BON allows 150, Smile allows 200, and Appstle allows 250. NUO AI states no cap on any of these.

Should I use a cart-level tiered rewards app or a loyalty points app?

Use cart-level tiers (NUO AI, Kite) to lift the current order's value with an immediate, visible threshold. Use a loyalty points app (Smile, BON, Appstle) to build repeat-purchase behavior over an account's history. The two are complementary, not competing — many stores eventually run both, but they solve different problems and shouldn't be compared as if interchangeable.

Do loyalty apps like Smile and BON also offer spend-threshold VIP tiers?

Yes, but the threshold is cumulative account spend over time, not a single cart total. A shopper might need to spend $500 across several orders to reach a VIP tier on these apps, versus crossing a $75 cart threshold once to unlock a cart-level reward on NUO AI or Kite.
