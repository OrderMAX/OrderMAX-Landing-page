Best frequently bought together apps for Shopify, compared

Four of the five apps that show up on every “best frequently bought together app” list have a free plan. The fifth doesn't. The four that do are capped in three different units — manual bundles, campaigns, and attributed revenue. Every figure below was read from that app’s own Shopify App Store listing on the same day and quoted verbatim.

The short answer

Frequently Bought Together CBB is the oldest dedicated FBT app, but its free plan caps at 3 manual bundles. Selleasy's free plan now caps at 5 campaigns with unlimited orders — a change from the order-based cap it used earlier in 2026. Essential ties its cap to $100 of attributed revenue. Fast Bundle, despite appearing on nearly every FBT roundup, restricts its free tier to development stores only. NUO AI, disclosed below, states no cap, with a much shorter review history than the other four.

What ends each free plan

One-click companion bundles on the product page and in the cart are close to table stakes across this category. The cap — or whether the free plan even applies to a live store — is what actually differs. Ratings and review counts are as shown on the Shopify App Store on 3 November 2026.

What this comparison does not tell you

Three things it deliberately does not claim, because the evidence for them does not exist:

It does not rank the apps by conversion or AOV lift. No one has run these five apps against the same catalog, traffic and bundle strategy. FBT's effect on AOV is well documented generally; this comparison cannot tell you which app's implementation performs best for your store.

It does not verify AI recommendation quality side by side. CBB and Fast Bundle both describe AI-driven pairing; the underlying models and training data were not compared. The caps were verifiable from each listing in an afternoon; recommendation quality was not.

Pricing goes stale, and currency display is regional. Everything here was read on 3 November 2026 — and Selleasy's own cap changed shape earlier this year, which is proof it can happen again. Frequently asked questions

Which Shopify frequently bought together apps actually have a free plan?

As of 3 November 2026, four of the five widely recommended FBT apps have a genuine $0 plan usable on a live store: NUO AI, Frequently Bought Together CBB, Selleasy and Essential Upsell. The fifth, Fast Bundle, restricts its free tier to development stores only.

What actually ends a free FBT app plan?

Three different units among the apps with a usable free plan. Frequently Bought Together CBB caps at 3 manual bundles. Selleasy caps at 5 campaigns, unlimited orders. Essential caps at $100 of attributed revenue. NUO AI states no cap. Fast Bundle has no usable free plan at all on a live store.

Has Selleasy's free plan changed recently?

Yes. As of 3 November 2026 the listing states the free plan allows "up to 5 campaigns" with unlimited orders and 18 upsell/cross-sell strategies — a campaign-count cap. Earlier in 2026 the stated cap was an order-count threshold instead. This is exactly why pricing comparisons go stale and should be re-read from the live listing rather than copied from an older roundup.

Is Fast Bundle's free plan usable on a real store?

No. Fast Bundle's listing states its free tier is for development stores only, and that unlimited AI-powered frequently bought together is included only on paid tiers starting around $19/month, capped by monthly bundle sales revenue rather than orders.

Which free FBT app has the most generous cap?

For a store running several bundle types at once, Selleasy's 5-campaign cap with unlimited orders tends to go furthest, since Frequently Bought Together CBB's 3-manual-bundle cap is tighter and Essential's $100 revenue cap expires fastest for a store where it's working well. NUO AI has no cap at all, with the trade-off of a much shorter review history.

Does frequently bought together use AI or manual pairing?

Both exist in this category. Frequently Bought Together CBB offers AI, manual or global recommendation modes even on its free plan. Fast Bundle's AI-powered FBT is paid-tier only. NUO AI uses AI-matched recommendations as part of its cart upsell engine across every plan, since there is only one plan.

Why does NUO AI appear in its own comparison?

Because leaving it out would be its own kind of bias. NUO AI publishes this comparison and is one of the five apps in it. Every competitor's pricing below was read from its own Shopify App Store listing on 3 November 2026 and quoted verbatim, and NUO AI's weakest point — a short review history against competitors with thousands — is stated rather than hidden.

How many App Store reviews does NUO AI have?

Far fewer than the other four apps on this page, which range from 2,481 to 3,642 reviews as of 3 November 2026. A merchant choosing on track record alone should weigh that heavily; this comparison does not argue otherwise.
